# WPA Living Historical Worlds — 2.3.7 (Alexander)

**Status:** SOURCE RELEASE CANDIDATE; not live, not a commercial authorisation.

## Implemented
- Deterministic geographic-themed artwork per level, with visibly differentiated gardens, hills, towns, rivers, oasis/desert and evening scenes, only where plausible. No false claim of exact historical reconstruction.
- Decorative birds, butterflies, foliage, fences, flowers, fountains and streams; gameplay physics unchanged.
- Original procedurally created sound effects: songbirds, brook, breeze and dusk insects, 20 s MP3 loops generated by `scripts/generate_original_ambience.py`. Audio is **synthetic sound design**, not a recording of a real nightingale or stream; no stock recordings or licensing sites used.
- Audio is muted on entry and plays only on explicit choice during active gameplay. User controls nature on/off, volume, and scenic motion separately. Retains settings locally. Pause/finish/tab-hidden suspends ambience. Music ducking prevents ambience from overpowering soundtrack.
- CSS animations respect reduced-motion; Phaser extra animations do not run when reduced-motion is requested. Graphics never have collision bodies or intercept touch inputs.
